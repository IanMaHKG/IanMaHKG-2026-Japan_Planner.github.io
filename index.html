Trip Overview 行程概覽

A balanced, relaxed journey through Japan's most iconic winter landscapes — designed for a family of three. 專為三人家庭設計，在東京、富士山及關西之間，享受平衡且悠閒的日本冬日旅程。

Pace 步調節奏

Balanced to relaxed. No more than 2–3 activities per day, with rest built in for your parents.

Practical Tips 實用出遊資訊

Everything you need to prepare before departure. 出發前必須做好的準備工作與交通卡券指南。

🚆

Transport Passes 交通卡券

  • Suica / PASMO card — Tap-and-go for all trains, buses, and convenience stores in Tokyo. Add to Apple/Google Wallet. Suica / PASMO 卡 — 適用於東京所有地鐵、巴士和便利店。可以直接加入 Apple/Google Wallet。
  • JR Kansai Area Pass (4-day) — Covers JR trains in Kyoto (京都), Osaka (大阪), Nara (奈良), Kobe (神戶). Buy online before departure for discount. JR 關西地區鐵路周遊券 (4日) — 覆蓋京都、大阪、奈良、神戶的 JR 火車。出發前網上購買享優惠。
  • Rental car — Book via Nissan or Toyota Rent-a-Car. Request studless winter tires + GPS with English. One-way drop-off fee applies (Tokyo → Nagoya ≈ ¥15,000–20,000). 自駕租車 — 建議選擇日產 (Nissan) 或豐田 (Toyota) 租車。必須指定配備雪地無釘輪胎 (Studless Winter Tires) 及英文 GPS。異地還車（東京借、名古屋還）需收約 ¥15,000–20,000 附加費。
  • International Driving Permit (IDP) — Obtain this in your home country before the trip. Required for all foreign drivers in Japan. 國際駕駛執照 (IDP) — 出發前必須在香港辦妥（運輸署申領）。在日本駕駛必須隨身攜帶。
🎫

Advance Bookings 景點與餐飲預約

  • Ryokan (Mt Fuji / Hakone) — Book 2–3 months ahead. Request "Fuji-view" room specifically. 溫泉旅館（富士山 / 箱根） — 必須提前 2 至 3 個月預訂。預訂時請特別註明需要「富士山景房 (Fuji-view room)」。
  • teamLab Borderless — Online tickets only, sell out fast. Book 2+ weeks ahead. teamLab 無界美術館 — 僅限網上預售門票，極快售罄。請至少提早 2 星期預購。
  • Restaurants — Reserve popular spots 3+ weeks ahead for Christmas week. Use Tabelog or ask your hotel concierge. 餐廳預約 — 聖誕黃金周期間，熱門餐廳必須提早 3 星期以上預約。可使用 Tabelog 網站或請酒店禮賓部協助。
  • Fushimi Inari (伏見稻荷大社) — Free entry, no reservation needed, but go early (before 9 AM) to beat crowds. 伏見稻荷大社 — 免費入場，無須預約，但建議早上 9 點前抵達以避開旅行團。
🙏

Etiquette & Culture 當地禮儀與習俗

  • Shoes off — Remove shoes when entering homes, ryokans, some restaurants, and temple halls. 脫鞋禮儀 — 進入傳統溫泉旅館、部分餐廳、日式民居及寺廟大殿時必須脫鞋。
  • Quiet trains — Keep phone on silent; avoid calls or loud conversations on trains. 乘車禮儀 — 乘搭火車及地鐵時，請將電話調至靜音模式，並避免大聲說話或講電話。
  • Onsen rules — Wash thoroughly before entering. Tattoos may need cover-up patches (ask beforehand). Towels stay out of the water. 溫泉守則 — 進入浴池前必須先在淋浴區徹底沖洗乾淨。如有紋身可能需要使用遮蓋貼紙（請先向旅館查詢）。毛巾切勿放入池水中。
  • Cash — Carry ¥10,000–20,000 in cash. Many small restaurants and temples are cash-only. 7-Eleven ATMs accept foreign cards. 現金準備 — 請隨身攜帶至少 ¥10,000–20,000 現金。許多小店、神社及寺廟只收現金。7-Eleven 的 ATM 可用外國信用卡提款。
  • Tipping — Never tip in Japan. It can be considered rude. 小費文化 — 日本完全沒有給小費的習慣，甚至會被視為不禮貌。
❄️

Winter Driving Safety 冬季自駕安全

  • Drive in daylight only — Sunset is ~16:30 in December. Plan arrivals before dark. 日光時間駕駛 — 12月日本約下午 4:30 便日落。請務必在天黑前抵達目的地旅館。
  • Check weather daily — If heavy snow is forecast, skip the drive and take a train instead. 每日留意天氣 — 如預測有大雪，應放棄自駕，改乘火車新幹線，安全至上。
  • Go slow on mountain roads — Extra caution on bridges, tunnels, and shaded curves where black ice forms. 山路減速慢行 — 橋樑、隧道出入口及背陰處容易形成肉眼難見的「黑冰 (Black Ice)」，須格外小心。
  • Konbini pit stops — 7-Eleven, Lawson, FamilyMart are everywhere. Great for snacks, bathrooms, and hot drinks. 便利店補給站 — 沿途的便利店是長途駕駛的最佳伴侶，可上洗手間、購買熱飲和點心。

Arrive at Narita (成田) / Haneda (羽田) Airport 抵達成田 / 羽田機場

Clear customs, pick up your Suica card at the airport station, and take the Narita Express or Limousine Bus to your hotel. Check in and rest after the flight.

辦理入境手續，在機場車站領取/購買 Suica 卡，乘搭成田特快 (N'EX) 或機場利木津巴士直達市區酒店。登記入住並稍作休息，舒緩飛行疲勞。

🍳 Breakfast / Brunch: Grab a quick meal at a konbini (便利店) (7-Eleven onigiri, sandwiches, and hot coffee) — surprisingly delicious and perfect after a long flight. 早餐 / 早午餐： 在機場或酒店附近的便利店 (Konbini) 買點輕食（7-Eleven 御飯糰、三文治和熱咖啡）—— 日式便利店食品水準極高，方便快捷。
🌤️ Afternoon 🌤️ 下午

Senso-ji Temple (淺草寺) & Nakamise-dori (仲見世通) 淺草寺與仲見世通

Tokyo's oldest temple. Walk through the iconic Kaminarimon (雷門 / Thunder Gate) and browse the traditional shopping street. Pick up souvenirs, taste ningyo-yaki (人形燒) (sweet bean cakes) and age-manju (炸饅頭) (fried pastries).

參訪東京最古老的寺廟。穿過寫有巨型紅燈籠的雷門，沿著仲見世通傳統商店街漫步。一邊選購特色手工藝品，一邊品嚐現烤的人形燒和酥脆的炸饅頭。

🍜 Lunch: Sometaro (染太郎) (Asakusa) — A charming, family-friendly spot where you cook your own okonomiyaki on a tabletop griddle. Fun and delicious. 午餐： 染太郎 (Sometaro) （淺草）— 懷舊的塌塌米老店，讓一家人圍著鐵板親自動手煎大阪燒/御好燒，既好玩又美味。
🌆 Evening 🌆 傍晚與晚上

Tokyo Skytree (東京晴空塔) & Solamachi (晴空街道) 東京晴空塔與 Solamachi 購物中心

Head to Tokyo Skytree (東京晴空塔) (634m) for sunset and city lights views. The Tembo Deck offers a 360° panorama. Browse Solamachi shopping complex below.

前往高 634 米的東京晴空塔。登上帝國觀景台 (Tembo Deck) 俯瞰 360 度東京大都會夕陽與無邊夜景。隨後可到塔下的 Solamachi 商場逛街購物。

🍱 Dinner: Asakusa Imahan (淺草今半) — A legendary sukiyaki restaurant (est. 1895). The A5 wagyu sukiyaki set is an unforgettable first-night-in-Japan dinner. Reserve ahead. 晚餐： 淺草今半 (Asakusa Imahan) — 創立於1895年的壽喜燒百年名店。精選頂級 A5 黑毛和牛壽喜燒套餐，為日本之旅第一晚留下最驚艷的味覺記憶。（必須提前預約）
💡 Tip: If arriving early, consider visiting Ueno Park (上野公園) (10 min from Asakusa) for a peaceful stroll by Shinobazu Pond. The Ameyoko market nearby is great for snacks. 💡 貼心提示： 若航班較早抵達，可先到鄰近的上野公園不忍池散步，或到旁邊的阿美橫丁 (Ameyoko) 感受熱鬧的傳統市場氣氛。

Meiji Shrine (明治神宮) & Harajuku (原宿) 明治神宮與原宿散策

Start with a serene walk through the forested approach to Meiji Shrine (明治神宮) — one of Tokyo's most important Shinto shrines. Then stroll Takeshita Street (竹下通) for Harajuku's famous youth culture and unique shops.

清晨漫步於明治神宮被林蔭環繞的碎石步道，感受鬧市中難得的莊嚴與寧靜。隨後步行前往潮流發源地原宿竹下通，欣賞獨特的日本街頭文化。

🍳 Breakfast: Bills Omotesando (表參道) — Famous for "the world's best scrambled eggs" and fluffy ricotta pancakes. A relaxed, Western-style brunch your parents will love. 早餐： Bills 表參道 (Omotesando) — 被譽為擁有「世界第一早餐」的美名。招牌香蕉蜂蜜法式熱班戟和極致香滑的炒蛋，非常適合帶父母享受精緻悠閒的早晨。
🌤️ Afternoon 🌤️ 下午

Shibuya Crossing (澀谷十字路口) & Shibuya Sky (澀谷 Sky) 澀谷十字路口與 Shibuya Sky 展望台

Experience the world's busiest pedestrian crossing from above at Shibuya Sky (230m rooftop observation). Then explore the vibrant streets below. Visit the Hachiko statue (忠犬八公像) for the classic photo op.

登上極具人氣的 Shibuya Sky 露天展望台（高 230 米），俯瞰全東京最繁忙的澀谷十字路口全景。下樓後可與地標忠犬八公像合照留念。

🍜 Lunch: Gyukatsu Motomura (牛かつ もと村) (Shibuya) — Deep-fried wagyu cutlet served with a hot stone to sear it to your liking. An absolute must-try. 午餐： 牛かつ もと村 (Gyukatsu Motomura) （澀谷）— 吉列炸牛排。外皮酥脆而內裡保持粉嫩的和牛排，讓您在個人小石板上親自加熱煎熟，口感極佳。
🌆 Evening 🌆 晚上

Omotesando Illuminations (表參道燈飾) & Roppongi (六本木) 表參道與六本木冬日璀璨燈飾

Walk along Omotesando (表參道) boulevard to see the elegant champagne-gold winter illuminations draped across the zelkova trees. Then head to Roppongi Hills Keyakizaka (六本木之丘櫸坂) for the stunning blue-and-white light tunnel.

沿著表參道寬敞的林蔭大道散步，欣賞冬日限定的香檳金路樹燈飾。隨後前往六本木櫸坂通，觀賞以藍白光芒點亮、直落東京鐵塔背景的絕美光之隧道。

🍱 Dinner: Gonpachi Nishi-Azabu (權八 西麻布) (the "Kill Bill" restaurant) — Beautiful traditional interiors, great soba noodles and yakitori. Fun atmosphere and foreigner-friendly. 晚餐： 權八 西麻布 (Gonpachi) — 《標殺令 (Kill Bill)》經典電影場景靈感來源地。極具傳統大氣的大正時期裝潢，提供優質的日式串燒、蕎麥麵與清酒，氣氛熱鬧且對遊客非常友善。
💡 Tip: Book Shibuya Sky tickets online 1–2 weeks ahead to skip the queue. Sunset time slot (~16:00) is best for both daylight and night views. 💡 貼心提示： Shibuya Sky 必須提前 1-2 星期上網搶購門票。最推介挑選下午 4 點左右的日落場次，能同時拍到日落與亮燈後的東京都夜景。

Tsukiji Outer Market (築地場外市場) 築地場外市場美食巡禮

The original Tokyo fish market's outer stalls are still bustling with fresh seafood, tamagoyaki (sweet egg omelette), and street food. Arrive by 8:30 AM for the best selection. Walk through the narrow lanes sampling as you go.

保留了傳統風貌的築地場外市場依然活力十足。清晨 8:30 抵達最能避開人潮。漫步於熙來攘往的小巷中，沿途品嚐現燒海鮮、玉子燒及新鮮刺身，這是最道地的東京早餐體驗。

🍳 Breakfast: Tsukiji market grazing — Must-try: fresh sushi at Sushi Dai (寿司大) or Daiwa Sushi (大和寿司), tamagoyaki from Yamazaki (山崎), and grilled scallops from street vendors. 早餐推薦： 築地市場掃街 —— 必嚐：壽司大或大和壽司的即製握壽司、山崎的微甜玉子燒，以及現烤的扇貝和生蠔。
🌤️ Afternoon 🌤️ 下午

teamLab Borderless (Azabudai Hills / 麻布台之丘) teamLab Borderless 沉浸式數碼藝術展

Immerse yourselves in the stunning teamLab Borderless digital art museum in Azabudai Hills. Rooms of flowing light, interactive waterfalls, and infinite crystal universes. It's magical for all ages — your parents will be mesmerized. Allow 2–3 hours.

前往東京新地標麻布台之丘，觀賞世界頂級的 teamLab Borderless 數位美術館。置身於流動的光影瀑布、無邊際的水晶宇宙與夢幻茶屋中。視覺震撼非常適合全家同樂。建議遊覽 2 至 3 小時。

🍜 Lunch: Azabudai Hills food hall — Upscale dining options right at the complex. Try Niku Kappo (肉割烹) for premium wagyu bowls, or Afuri (阿夫利) for their signature yuzu-shio (yuzu salt) ramen. 午餐： 麻布台之丘地下美食廣場 —— 選擇多元且環境極佳。推介品嚐 肉割烹 (Niku Kappo) 的頂級和牛丼，或 AFURI (阿夫利) 招牌清爽的柚子鹽拉麵。
🌆 Evening 🌆 晚上

Tokyo Tower (東京鐵塔) at Night 東京鐵塔浪漫夜景

A 15-minute walk from Azabudai Hills. See Tokyo Tower lit up in its iconic orange glow — especially beautiful in winter. Go up to the Main Deck (150m) for city views, or simply admire it from Shiba Park (芝公園) below.

從麻布台之丘步行約 15 分鐘即可抵達。冬日裡散發溫暖橘紅色光芒的東京鐵塔是永恆的經典。可以選擇登上 150 米的主觀景台看夜景，或者在塔下的芝公園草地上拍照，感受老東京的復古浪漫情懷。

🍱 Dinner: Ukai Toriyama (うかい鳥山) (near Tokyo Tower) — A refined charcoal-grilled chicken restaurant in a beautiful traditional garden setting. Perfect for a special dinner. Or go casual at T's TanTan in nearby Tokyo Station for outstanding vegan ramen. 晚餐： 東京芝豆腐屋吉兆 / 鵜飼鳥山 (Ukai) —— 位於鐵塔腳下，隱密於傳統日式庭園中的高級炭火燒烤與豆腐料理名店，環境極美，是帶父母享用奢華晚餐的不二之選。（必須提前預約）
💡 Tip: teamLab Borderless tickets sell out quickly — book online at least 2 weeks ahead! Choose a weekday afternoon slot for fewer crowds. 💡 貼心提示： teamLab 門票必須提前於官網預約，不設現場售票。館內有許多鏡面地板，建議避免穿裙子前往。

Imperial Palace East Gardens (皇居東御苑) 皇居外苑與東御苑

Free entry to the serene gardens of the Imperial Palace. Beautifully maintained lawns with historic stone walls, moats, and seasonal plantings. Flat, easy walking — perfect for parents. Walk to the Nijubashi Bridge (二重橋) for the classic photo op.

New Year's Eve — Toshikoshi Soba (跨年蕎麥麵) & Temple Bells (除夜之鐘) 日本大年夜大晦日 —— 跨年蕎麥麵與除夜之鐘

Celebrate a traditional Japanese NYE! The local tradition is to eat toshikoshi soba (年越し蕎麥) (year-crossing buckwheat noodles) for health and longevity. At midnight, visit Shitennoji Temple (四天王寺) to hear the bells ring 108 times to wash away the 108 earthly desires.

🍱 Dinner: Toshikoshi soba — Join the locals! Visit any traditional soba noodle shop (like Matsuya) for hot tempura soba. Alternatively, dine at Mizuno (美津の) for Osaka's best okonomiyaki. 晚餐： 除夕跨年蕎麥麵 —— 入鄉隨俗，到心齋橋的麵館（如松屋）吃一碗熱氣騰騰的炸蝦蕎麥麵。或在道頓堀的 美津の (Mizuno) 享用招牌山藥大阪燒，暖烘烘地迎接新年。
